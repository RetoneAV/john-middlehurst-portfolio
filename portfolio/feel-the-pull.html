Feel The Pull

Interactive Art Installation

FEEL THE PULL was an immersive installation at Clerkenwell Design Week exploring magnetic forces through light, sound and movement. Using skeletal tracking cameras and proximity sensors, it responded to visitors in real time — move closer and the visuals shifted, distorted and redirected; step back and the symmetry returned. More than 4,500 people came through, many calling it hypnotic. I programmed and developed the visuals, audio, lighting, and interactivity in TouchDesigner.
