Holodeck 3D Room

Interactive Art installation

What if your screen wasn’t a wall, but a window?

We’re moving past flat content. With 3D Parallax Rooms, the boundary between the physical and digital worlds is officially blurring.

Programmed in Touchdesigner, a Kinect sensor tracks your head position in 3D space, updating in real-time the parallax perspective of a 3D depth image. This gives an intense illusion of 3D depth into a flat panel screen, projection or LED wall, which as the user moves around allows them to see into another world.
