Outernet London

Data Visualisation

Commissioned by Pixel Artworks for a cryptography symposium at Outernet London, this series of data visualisations explored prime numbers at architectural scale. In TouchDesigner I used Python to generate mathematically accurate views of prime-number grids, prime spirals, elliptic curves, and complex modular forms, then plotted and animated them in 3D. The work ran across the large LED screens covering the walls and ceiling of Outernet’s Now Building and Arcade tunnel.
