Control The Swarm

Interactive Exhibition

Ever wanted to control a force of nature with a wave of your hand? 🌊✨

Meet “Control The Swarm”—an interactive experience where human movement controls digital chaos.

In a world of passive screens, how do you truly capture someone’s attention? You give them control.

Using a Kinect sensor to track arm and hand positions in 3D space, I created a particle system that behaves like a living, breathing fluid. As you move, the “swarm” reacts in real-time, flowing with your gestures and responding to the physics of your body.

It’s more than just a visual; it’s a physical extension of the user into a digital environment.
