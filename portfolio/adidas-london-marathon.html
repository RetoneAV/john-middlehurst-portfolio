Adidas London Marathon

Interactive Brand Activation

Part of adidas’s Adizero: For The Fast hub for London Marathon weekend, this installation invited runners to step onto a treadmill and try to hold world-record pace. Real-time speed, distance, and cadence filled the LED booth, with a live chase avatar, a public leaderboard, and a prize draw for a place at the Berlin Marathon. I developed the interactive treadmill system that drove the visual stat displays, chase avatar, and leaderboard, together with a bespoke audio control system.
